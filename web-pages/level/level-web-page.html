The Level bowling ball is the groundbreaking debut of the Lab Series, a line designed to redefine performance through limitless innovation. Wrapped around the new Level is a new coverstock material that has physical properties never seen before in the bowling industry. This makes the Level a great choice when consistent, continuous, predictable ball motion is needed.

BRAND NEW COVERSTOCK

RPM SOLID™

Featuring the groundbreaking RPM (Regenerative Polymer Matrix) coverstock, this revolutionary material introduces physical properties never before seen in the bowling industry, delivering consistent ball motion with reduced sensitivity to friction. Unlike traditional coverstocks that degrade over time, RPM preserves its surface integrity, ensuring a predictable reaction on every shot. Designed for bowlers seeking top-tier performance, the Level offers unmatched consistency, control, and cutting-edge innovation for the modern game.

POWERED BY

A.I. CORE™

At its core is the Equalizer A.I. Core™, a tall, low-density weight block engineered to deliver a higher RG for a smoother, more controlled transition without sacrificing differential or consistency. This precision design ensures unparalleled repeatability and shot-to-shot reliability.

BRAND NEW WEIGHTBLOCK

EQUALIZER™

The most crucial part of the design is the overall density of the weight block itself. A lighter density was chosen in order to increase the RG value so the Equalizer™ A.I. Core™ would take longer to respond to friction. Lighter densities raise the RG, but they simultaneously decrease the total differential. Traditionally, a shape of this design would require a higher density slug to increase differential into a zone that would be more manageable for a majority of bowlers. The cuts towards the middle of the shape combined with the increased overall length of the weight block create more differential than would normally be seen with a shape of this size and density.
