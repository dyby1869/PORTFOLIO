WHERE I COME FROM

I grew up in Satellite Beach, Florida, a small surf town where life revolved around the ocean. That’s where I learned to surf, and later how to chase flow in everything I do. I still carry that mindset today, whether I’m designing, snowboarding, or building something new.



I was lucky enough to take snowboard trips as a kid, and those experiences ended up shaping a huge part of my life. I still ride every winter here in Utah, where I now live and work for Storm Bowling, the company behind the sport I competed in growing up. I started bowling competitively at 13 and trained weekly for over a decade, learning what consistency and discipline really mean.

I studied UX Design at SCAD and later earned a UX certification from Springboard, where I built a foundation in research, design systems, and interaction design. Since then, I’ve combined that background with front-end development to design and build interactive experiences for Storm.

I care about clarity and flow, making sure every page I design not only looks good but works intuitively. My goal is always the same: to make the web feel fast, focused, and alive.

Outside of work, I’m either skating, snowboarding, or finding new ways to push myself creatively and physically. Staying active and curious has always gone hand in hand for me. Both keep me learning and moving forward.

I’m currently open to freelance collaborations, contract roles, or full-time opportunities focused on UX, interaction, or front-end design.
