BEHIND THE WORK

Hey I’m Dylan. I’m a designer who codes. I combine user-centered research with custom-built front-end experiences. I studied UX Design at SCAD, earned a UX certification from Springboard, and since then I’ve kept my process sharp by doing both: conducting the research and building the interface. That loop from insight to execution is where I work best.



Over the last few years I’ve been building and improving the website for Storm Bowling, including animated layouts, filtering systems, and interactive product experiences, all grounded in direct user research. I care about clarity, flow, and making sure design decisions are backed by insights, not guesses.

My approach is hands-on: I dive into the why behind the experience, then bring it to life with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (GSAP is a favorite). I work with what the data says to build and design better.

Outside of design, I chase clean lines on a snowboard, on a wave, or on a Figma file. I’m always up for a chat if you would like to reach out.

Currently open to projects, freelance collaborations, or roles where research, design, and code meet.
