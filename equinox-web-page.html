Located on top, the second-largest cylinder introduces versatility through drilling manipulation. By adjusting the drilling depth, bowlers can fine-tune the ball’s reaction to suit their individual needs. This design element ensures the EquinoX adapts to different styles while maintaining precision and control.

Positioned along the Z-axis, the largest cylinder in the Solarion A.I. Core plays a pivotal role in lowering the ball’s RG, allowing it to rev quickly and gain momentum as it travels down the lane. This unique positioning also creates a tumbling effect through the front of the lane, setting up a strong foundation for consistent ball motion and explosive pin action.

The smallest cylinder, positioned at the base of the core, is paired with a lighter slug to enhance torque and extend the core’s height. This innovative structure increases rotational energy downlane, delivering aggressive continuation and a sharp entry angle off the breakpoint for the powerful backend motion bowlers demand.
